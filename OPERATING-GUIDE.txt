This file explains how to run and use the software. It is in two parts.

  PART 1 is for staff. How to sign in and do your daily work.
  PART 2 is for whoever runs the system. Setup, maintenance, and fixing things.

Read the part that applies to you. Part 3 lists rules the software enforces,
and Part 4 is an honest list of what is not finished.

If you just want to log in and look around, go straight to section 2.12. It has
test logins for every job in the business.


===============================================================================
WHAT THIS SOFTWARE IS
===============================================================================

It is an app for running the sales side of the business. It covers:

  - Staff attendance, with GPS
  - Customer visits and orders taken in the field
  - The full path an order takes: approval, picking, checking, billing,
    dispatch, delivery
  - Purchases and goods coming in from suppliers
  - Payments, cheques, and the daily cash close
  - Salary, advances, leave, and sales incentives
  - Reports
  - Sending the accounts across to Tally

It runs on phones, tablets, and normal computers through a web browser. The
same app adjusts itself to the screen size. On a computer you get a menu down
the left side. On a phone you get buttons along the bottom.

There are two pieces:

  1. The app itself, which people use.
  2. The server, which holds all the data. It talks to a MySQL database.

The app is useless without the server. There is no offline mode. If the server
cannot be reached, the app says so instead of pretending to work.

PART 1 - FOR STAFF
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 SIGNING IN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open the app. You will see a login box asking for a username and password.

Your username is your first name in small letters. For example: monu, gaurav,
ashish, sibu.

Your password is given to you by the owner. It is not written down anywhere in
the app and nobody can look it up for you.

If you type the wrong password the app just says the login failed. It will not
tell you whether the username exists or whether it was the password that was
wrong. This is on purpose, so that a stranger cannot use the login screen to
find out who works here.

If you get it wrong several times in a row, the app stops accepting attempts
for a short while. Wait a few minutes and try again.

Forgot your password? Ask Yash. He can set a new one for you.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 THE FIRST TIME YOU SIGN IN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The first time you sign in, the app will ask you to choose your own password
before it lets you do anything else.

This happens because the password you were given was chosen by somebody else,
so more than one person knows it. Until you pick your own, you cannot open any
other screen. There is no way to skip it and no "remind me later".

You need:

  - The password you just signed in with
  - A new password of your own, at least 8 characters
  - The new password typed a second time to confirm it

Simple passwords like "password123" are refused. Pick something only you know.

The same thing happens again if Yash ever resets your password for you. That is
normal.

If you are on the wrong account, there is a Sign Out button on that screen.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 STARTING YOUR DAY - CHECKING IN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Everybody checks in once a day.

When you open the app at the start of your shift it will ask you to check in.
It needs your location, so:

  - Turn on GPS / location on your phone
  - Allow the app to use your location when it asks
  - Be outside or near a window if the signal is weak

The app will not let you check in without a real GPS reading. It does not guess
your location. If it cannot get a fix, it says so. Move somewhere with a better
signal and try again.

There are two shifts:

  Shift A   starts 10:00 am   ends 6:45 pm
  Shift B   starts 11:00 am   ends 8:00 pm

You get 10 minutes of grace. Shift A staff checking in after 10:10 am are
marked late. Shift B after 11:10 am.

If you are more than an hour late, the day counts as absent.

If you check out before 6:00 pm (Shift A) or 7:00 pm (Shift B), it is a half
day.

During the day:

  Lunch Out    tap when you leave for lunch
  Lunch In     tap when you come back
  Check Out    tap at the end of your shift

If you forget to check out, the system closes your shift automatically later
that night. It will be marked as an automatic checkout so it is clear what
happened.

While you are checked in and out of the shop, the app quietly records your
location every few minutes. This stops when you check out. This is for the
company's delivery and visit records. It does not run on the web version, only
on a phone.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4 IF YOU ARE A SALESMAN (Monu, Prasenjit, Manish)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your main screen is called Today. It shows your visits and your figures for the
day.

TAKING AN ORDER

  1. Tap Take Order.
  2. Pick the customer. If they are new, tap New Dealer first and add them.
  3. Add the items and the quantity for each.
  4. Save.

The price is worked out by the server, not by you. You cannot type a rate. The
rate depends on what kind of customer they are - a dealer pays a different
price from a retail customer. This is why the customer type matters so much.

If the app refuses the order and says the customer has no type set, that
customer has not been classified yet. Tell Yash or Gaurav. Do not guess.

If it says an item is not rate carded, that item has no price set up yet. It
cannot be sold until Gaurav puts a price on it.

A VISIT THAT PRODUCES NO ORDER

Record it as a no-order visit. Do not create an empty order and cancel it.
These two things are counted differently in the reports.

ESTIMATES

You can make an estimate (a quote) instead of an order, and turn it into a real
order later if the customer agrees.

COLLECTIONS

If you collect cash or cheques during the day, use Hand in collections at the
bottom of your Today screen. Enter what you are carrying before you hand it
over. Sibu counts it separately and enters what he actually received. The two
numbers are compared.

Do not skip the declaration. If the counts do not match, the declaration is the
only proof of what you said you had.

OTHER THINGS ON YOUR SCREEN

  Beat        your planned route of customer visits
  Estimate    make a quote
  Scheme      the electrician scheme slabs and running totals


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.5 IF YOU APPROVE ORDERS (Manas)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your main screen is Orders. It lists everything waiting for approval.

Tap an order to see the customer, the lines, and the value. Then approve or
reject it.

Nothing moves until you approve. The picker cannot start, so an order sitting
here is an order not being packed.

Nobody can approve an order they raised themselves. If you raise one, somebody
else has to approve it.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.6 IF YOU PICK (Ashish, Rajesh, Hirak, Ganesh, Prabal)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your main screen is Pick. It shows approved orders waiting to be picked.

  1. Tap an order.
  2. Before you can record anything, the app asks you to confirm you have
     written the SO number in the godown register. Tick it. This is a paper
     record the app cannot check for you, so it just makes sure the step is
     not skipped and records that you said you did it.
  3. Go through each line and enter how many you actually picked.
  4. If you cannot find enough stock, enter the smaller number. Do not enter
     the ordered quantity and sort it out later.
  5. Hand over when done.

A short pick is fine. The customer gets billed for what was actually picked,
not what was ordered. That only works if you enter the true number.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.7 IF YOU CHECK AND DISPATCH (Ajit)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You have two screens.

VERIFY

Picked orders come to you to be counted. You count every item against what the
picker said they picked - not against the original order. Enter your count.

If your count is lower than the picker's, that is recorded. This is the whole
point of the step, so enter what you actually count.

Billing will not happen until you have verified. That is enforced by the
server, not just hidden in the app.

DISPATCH

Build the delivery sheet. Choose which driver takes which orders. You need the
staff list for this, which you have.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.8 IF YOU BILL (Gaurav)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your main screen is Invoice.

Verified orders come to you. Make the invoice. The invoice bills the counted
quantity, so if the pick was short, the bill is short too.

The printed invoice comes out as three copies in one file - Original,
Duplicate, Triplicate. Print all three together. The Duplicate is the one that
comes back signed.

The agent's name and commission never appear on the invoice. The customer must
not see what their agent is paid.

CREDIT NOTES AND RETURNS

The Credit screen handles goods coming back. Record what came back and whether
it is saleable.

RATES

You are the only person who can change an item's rates. But a change you make
is a request, not a change. It waits for Yash or Manoj to approve it. Until
they do, the old rate is still what customers are billed.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.9 IF YOU DRIVE (Kamal, Siva)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your main screen is Route. It shows your stops for the day.

At each stop:

  1. Tap the stop.
  2. Enter the name of the person who received the goods.
  3. Take a photo. This is required. You cannot close the stop without it.
  4. Save.

If the delivery fails, record it as undelivered and pick a reason. It goes back
on a sheet for another attempt. It does not disappear.

There is no signature to collect. The photo and the name are the proof.

Your Done screen shows what you have already delivered.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.10 IF YOU BUY (Sonu)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your main screen is Purchase.

RECORDING A PURCHASE

Pick the type first, because it changes what is required:

  Local - Bill        a proper GST bill from a local supplier
  Local - Challan     a challan now, GST bill to follow within 7 days
  Company Direct      straight from the company
  Outside - Bill      GST bill from outside Guwahati
  Outside - Challan   challan from outside, GST bill to follow

For every line you enter two numbers:

  Bill quantity     what the supplier's paper says
  Actual quantity   what you actually counted

These are separate on purpose. Stock moves on what you counted. If they differ,
the difference is flagged as a shortage or an excess so somebody chases it. Do
not copy one into the other.

A photo of the goods received is required.

GOODS IN TRANSIT

Reached from the Purchase screen. This is for goods that have been sent but
have not arrived yet.

When a bilty (LR) arrives, record it: the LR number, supplier, transporter,
expected date, and freight. The app works out an expected arrival from how long
that supplier usually takes.

As the consignment moves, update it:

  In Guwahati        transport says it has arrived in town
  Received           goods are physically here
  Short / damaged    something is wrong - you must type what

Anything past its expected date shows as late. The owner is told automatically
after 2 days, and again after 5.

The same screen shows challan purchases still waiting for their GST bill, with
how many days are left. When the bill arrives, tap Bill in and enter the
number.

INTERNAL TRANSFERS

Also from the Purchase screen. For stock moving between the shop and the
godown. The person sending records what was sent. A different person at the
other end counts what arrived and enters that number. The sender cannot receive
their own transfer.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.11 IF YOU HANDLE CASH (Sibu, Damodar)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SIBU - END OF DAY

Your main screen is EOD.

Collections handed in today shows what each salesman declared. For each one,
count the money yourself and enter what you counted. Do not copy their number.

If your count and their declaration disagree, the app records both and marks it
disputed. It does not pick a side.

Then close the day. Enter the counted cash. If it does not match what the
system expects, it tells you the difference. Recount before closing, because
the close is recorded against your name.

CHEQUES

The Cheques screen tracks cheques from receipt to clearing.

DAMODAR - CHEQUE DEPOSIT

You take cheques to the bank and upload a photo of the deposit slip. The photo
is required.

If a cheque bounces, record it. The system automatically undoes the payment,
puts the invoice back to unpaid, and cancels any cash discount that was given
on it.

PAYMENTS

When money comes in, it is applied to the oldest unpaid invoice first. This is
automatic. You cannot point a payment at a chosen invoice, because that is how
old debts stay old.

Customers who pay quickly earn a discount:

  Paid within 2 days      2.5 percent
  Paid within 3-10 days   2 percent
  Paid within 11-20 days  1 percent

This applies to dealers.

YOU ALSO APPROVE

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.13 YOUR PAY - EVERYBODY HAS THIS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go to the Profile tab. Under "Your pay" there are three screens.

SALARY

Shows this month: your fixed salary, and everything taken off it.

While the month is still running the figures move, because they are worked out
from your attendance as it happens. Once the month is finalised the numbers are
frozen and you can open a printable salary slip.

Deductions are listed one by one with the date and the reason, so you can see
exactly why the total is less than your fixed salary.

Your daily rate is your salary divided by 26 working days.

ADVANCES AND LEAVE

Two tabs on one screen.

To ask for an advance: enter the amount and how many months to repay it over.
The screen shows what will come off each month before you send it. It waits for
Yash or Manoj.

To apply for leave: enter the dates and a reason.

Apply for leave in advance if you can. Approved leave costs you one day's pay.
Being absent without telling anyone costs two.

INCENTIVE

Shows your progress across twenty product segments. Each one has a target and a
bar showing how close you are.

How it pays:

  Below 90 percent of target      nothing
  90 to 99 percent                80 percent of the base incentive
  100 percent                     the full base incentive
  101 percent and above           110 percent - this is the maximum

Going far past target does not pay more than 110 percent.

Watch for the line that says "removed - unpaid beyond 60 days". If a customer
you sold to has not paid after 60 days, that sale is taken off your
achievement. Collecting the money brings it back. Sometimes chasing a payment
is worth more to you than chasing a new order.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 WHAT YOU NEED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Node.js version 18 or newer
  - MySQL or MariaDB (XAMPP is what was used in development)
  - A computer that stays on, for the server
  - Tally Prime on an office machine, if you want the accounts sync

The app and the server can be on the same machine to start with.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 FIRST TIME SETUP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SHORT WAY - TWO COMMANDS

Start MySQL first (in XAMPP, press Start next to MySQL). Then open a command
window in the project folder and run:

  npm install
  npm run dev

That is the whole setup. npm run dev works out what has not been done yet and
does it: writes backend/.env with a generated JWT_SECRET, creates the database
and its 72 tables, creates the staff accounts and an administrator, loads the
items and prices from the two spreadsheets, loads the incentive segments, then
starts the server and the app together in the one terminal.

It prints the login password when it finishes.

Everything it does is skipped if already done, so npm run dev is also how you
start work every day afterwards. On a machine that is already set up it prints
a few ok lines and starts straight away.

If something is missing it stops and names the fix rather than showing an
error. The commonest one is MySQL not being started.

To do the setting up without starting anything:

  npm run setup

THE LONG WAY - ONE STEP AT A TIME

You do not need this if the short way worked. It is here because it is useful
to know what the short way is actually doing, and because you may want to do
one part differently - a different admin username, for instance, or your own
password instead of the shared development one.

Open a command window in the project folder.

STEP 1 - Install

  npm install
  cd backend
  npm install

STEP 2 - Settings

In the backend folder, copy .env.example to .env and edit it. The important
ones:

  DB_HOST, DB_PORT, DB_USER, DB_PASSWORD, DB_NAME
        How to reach your database.

  JWT_SECRET
        A long random string. This signs the login tokens. Make up something
        long and random. Do not leave it blank and do not share it.

  PORT
        The port the server listens on. 5000 by default.

  BUSINESS_TIMEZONE
        Asia/Kolkata. Leave it.

  CORS_ORIGIN
        Which web addresses may talk to the server. In .env.example this line
        is commented out with a # at the front, which means every address is
        accepted. That is fine on the office network. Before you put this on
        the internet, remove the # and list your real addresses.

  AUTO_CHECKOUT_TIME
        When forgotten shifts are closed automatically.

  LOCATION_RETENTION_DAYS
        How long GPS records are kept.

  TALLY_ENABLED
        false unless you have set up Tally. See section 2.8.

  GEOCODE_ENABLED
        false by default. Turning it on sends staff GPS coordinates to an
        outside company to turn them into street addresses. That is a business
        decision about staff privacy. Think about it before switching it on.

STEP 3 - Create the database

  cd backend
  npm run init-db

This creates the database and all the tables. It puts no business data in.

STEP 4 - Create the first admin account

  npm run create-admin -- --id ADMIN001 --name "Your Name" --email you@example.com --password "at-least-8-chars"

STEP 5 - Load the staff accounts

  npm run seed-business

This creates the 22 staff accounts with their shifts and permissions. It prints
a one-time password for each account.

  *** COPY THOSE PASSWORDS NOW. They are not saved anywhere and cannot be
  *** recovered. Each person must change theirs the first time they sign in.

STEP 6 - Load the item rates

  npm run import-rates -- --all --dry-run
  npm run import-rates -- --all

The first command shows what it would do without doing it. The second does it.
This reads the two spreadsheets. It never writes to them.

STEP 7 - Load the incentive segments

  npm run seed-segments

STEP 8 - Start the server

  npm run dev

Check it is alive:

  curl localhost:5000/health        is the server running
  curl localhost:5000/health/db     can it reach the database

STEP 9 - Start the app

From the main project folder, in a second command window:

  npm run dev        for phones and tablets, through Expo
  npm run web        to open it in a web browser

On a phone, install Expo Go and scan the code. The app finds the server
automatically from the machine that served it.

2.3 EVERYDAY COMMANDS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All of these are run from the backend folder.

STARTING AND STOPPING

  npm run dev              start the server, restarting when files change
  npm start                start the server without the restarting

HOUSEKEEPING

  npm run auto-checkout -- --dry-run    show shifts that would be closed
  npm run auto-checkout                 close them
  npm run purge-locations -- --dry-run  show old GPS records that would go
  npm run purge-locations               delete them

The server already runs auto-checkout and alerts every hour on its own. The
commands above are for running them by hand.

Purging locations cannot be undone, and those records may matter in a pay
dispute. Check the dry run first.

CHECKING THE DATA IS CONSISTENT

  npm run recompute -- --dry-run   report any drift in the cached totals
  npm run recompute                fix it

Run this after anything unusual. Stock levels and customer balances are
calculated from movement records; this checks the two agree.

ACCOUNTS

  npm run secure-accounts -- --dry-run   who is still on a given password
  npm run secure-accounts                make them change it

  npm run seed-roles                     create any missing staff accounts
  npm run seed-business -- --reset       reapply shifts and permissions
                                         (never touches passwords)

TESTS

  npm run test:all       runs everything - 328 checks

The server must be running for the full set. If something breaks after a
change, run this first.

2.4 ADDING AND REMOVING STAFF
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ADDING SOMEONE

In the app, go to Profile, then People and access. Add the person, give them a
username, and type a password for them.

They will have to change that password the first time they sign in. This is
automatic. Tell them the password verbally or in person - do not message it.

Then tick the boxes for what they are allowed to do.

REMOVING SOMEONE

Do not delete the account. Set it to inactive.

Deleting a user destroys every attendance record and GPS record for that
person. Setting them inactive stops them signing in immediately and can be
undone. There is deliberately no bulk delete anywhere in the software.

RESETTING SOMEBODY'S PASSWORD

Edit the user and type a new password. They will be made to change it at their
next sign-in.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5 PERMISSIONS - HOW THEY WORK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What somebody can do is decided only by the boxes ticked on their account. Job
titles mean nothing to the software.

Permissions come in two sizes:

  A whole area, like "orders"
        Lets them see and act on everybody's orders.

  A single action, like "orders.view"
        Lets them see only their own.

This is how a salesman sees his own order book while a supervisor sees the
whole branch. Giving somebody the whole area widens what they see, not just
what they can do. Be careful with it.

The tabs a person sees are worked out from their permissions. Take a permission
away and the tab goes with it. There is no separate menu to edit.

Two permissions look similar and are not:

  items.rates      can SEE prices. Every salesman needs this to quote.
  items.pricing    can CHANGE prices. Only Gaurav should have this.

Sonu maintains the item list but deliberately cannot see prices.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.6 PRICES AND RATES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

An item does not have one price. It has six, one for each kind of customer:

  dealer
  retail_direct
  retail_commission
  electrician_direct
  builder_direct
  builder_commission

Which one a customer is billed at comes from their customer type. This is why
a customer with no type set cannot be sold to at all - the software refuses
rather than guessing, because guessing wrong can mean selling at dealer price
by accident.

Prices come from the two spreadsheets. To load a new rate card, replace the
file and run the import again. The app never writes to those files.

Changing a single price is done in the app by Gaurav, and needs Yash or Manoj
to approve it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.8 TALLY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The software can send invoices, receipts and vouchers to Tally Prime.

IMPORTANT: this has never been tested against a real Tally. It has only been
tested against a stand-in program. Expect to spend half a day on it the first
time. Everything below is written and ready, but unproven.

TO SET IT UP

  1. On the machine running Tally, open Tally and press F1, then Advanced
     Configuration. Set "Act as Server" to Yes, on port 9000.
  2. In backend/.env, fill in TALLY_HOST, TALLY_PORT, and TALLY_COMPANY.
     TALLY_COMPANY must match the company name EXACTLY as Tally shows it.
  3. Fill in the ledger names: TALLY_SALES_LEDGER, TALLY_PURCHASE_LEDGER,
     TALLY_CASH_LEDGER, TALLY_BANK_LEDGER, and the GST ledgers. These must
     already exist in Tally.
  4. Run the check:

       npm run tally -- --doctor

     This runs six checks and tells you exactly what to fix for each failure.
     Keep fixing and rerunning until everything passes.

  5. Only then set TALLY_ENABLED=true.

RUNNING IT

  npm run tally             send everything waiting, once
  npm run tally -- --watch  keep running and send continuously
  npm run tally -- --ping   is Tally reachable
  npm run tally -- --pull   also fetch customer and item lists from Tally

HOW IT BEHAVES

Documents are queued the moment they happen and sent when Tally is reachable.
If Tally is closed for the night, nothing is lost. It goes when Tally comes
back.

Stock levels and customer balances are compared with Tally, never overwritten.
If they disagree, it is reported as something for a person to look at. This is
deliberate. Overwriting would mean no figure in the app could be explained from
its own records afterwards.

WATCH OUT FOR

If a push reports "created nothing, altered nothing, no error", the company
name is almost certainly wrong. That is what Tally does when it does not
recognise the company.

Fixing TALLY_COMPANY does not fix documents already queued. The company name is
stored inside each queued document. Those have to be queued again.

The Tally Sync screen in the app shows all of this without needing the command
line.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.9 BEFORE PUTTING THIS ON THE INTERNET
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These are fine on an office network and wrong anywhere else.

  1. RETIRE THE OLD PASSWORDS. Run:

       npm run secure-accounts -- --dry-run
       npm run secure-accounts

     Older databases have staff accounts sharing one password that was written
     in the setup files, which means it is not private. This marks them so
     everybody must choose their own. Warn staff first, or it looks like the
     app is broken.

  2. Stop using the MySQL root user. Make a limited one:

       CREATE USER 'kl_app'@'localhost' IDENTIFIED BY 'something-long-random';
       GRANT SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE ON kl_electricals.* TO 'kl_app'@'localhost';

  3. Set CORS_ORIGIN to your actual web address. Blank means anyone can
     connect.

  4. Put it behind HTTPS. Right now the connection is not encrypted.

  5. For the Google Play Store, you need a screen explaining background
     location tracking before you ask for it. Google requires this.

The server warns you about items 2 and 3 when it starts.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.10 BACKUPS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Back up the MySQL database. Everything is in there.

Also back up backend/uploads, which holds delivery photos and deposit slips.
These are not in the database.

Do this daily. There is no built-in backup - use mysqldump or a scheduled task.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.11 WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NOBODY CAN SIGN IN

Check the server is running:      curl localhost:5000/health
Check it reaches the database:    curl localhost:5000/health/db

If the second one fails, MySQL is down or the .env settings are wrong.

ONE PERSON CANNOT SIGN IN

Check their account is active. Check they are typing the right username - it is
their first name in small letters. If they have failed several times, wait a
few minutes for the lockout to clear.

SOMEBODY IS STUCK ON THE CHANGE PASSWORD SCREEN

That is correct behaviour. They must choose a new password. If they cannot
remember the one they were given, reset it for them and give them the new one.

A SCREEN IS MISSING FOR SOMEBODY

They do not have the permission. Profile, People and access, tick the box.

THE APP SAYS AN ITEM HAS NO RATE

That item has no price set up. Gaurav must set one. Most of the item list is
in this state - see Part 4.

THE APP SAYS A CUSTOMER HAS NO TYPE

Somebody must classify that customer as dealer, retail, builder and so on.
Until then they cannot be sold to.

STOCK NUMBERS LOOK WRONG

  npm run recompute -- --dry-run

This compares the stored totals with the movement records and reports any
difference. Run without --dry-run to fix.

AFTER CHANGING ANYTHING IN THE CODE

  npm run migrate
  npm run rebuild-schema
  npm run build-init-sql
  npm run test:schema

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.13 SENDING THE PROJECT TO SOMEBODY ELSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

READ THIS FIRST: THE DATA IS NOT IN THE FOLDER

The project folder holds the program. It does not hold the database.

Your items, customers, orders, staff and attendance all live inside MySQL,
which keeps them in its own folder somewhere else on the machine (with XAMPP,
under xampp\mysql\data). Zipping the project folder does not pick any of that
up.

So whoever you send it to gets working software with an empty database. For
most purposes that is exactly what you want. If you actually need them to have
your data as well, see "Sending your data too" at the end of this section.

WHAT TO LEAVE OUT OF THE ZIP

Four things must not go in.

  node_modules        Two of these, one at the top and one in backend. They
                      come to 438 MB on this machine - 391 MB plus 47 MB. It
                      is downloaded code, some of it built for this exact
                      computer. The other person rebuilds it with one
                      command. Sending it can actually break their install
                      rather than help.

  backend\.env        Your database password and your JWT_SECRET. The
                      JWT_SECRET signs login tokens, so anybody who has it can
                      forge a login. Never send this file. They make their own
                      from .env.example.

  backend\uploads     Delivery photographs and cheque deposit slips. Right now
                      there are 160 files in there. These are pictures of real
                      customers' premises and real banking paperwork. Do not
                      hand them over.

  .expo               Local cache. Useless to anyone else.

Everything else goes.

MAKING THE ZIP

On Windows, open PowerShell in the folder ABOVE the project. These two commands
copy the project without the four items above, then zip the copy.

  robocopy "KL-electricals-2026-08-26" "KL-app-to-send" /E /XD node_modules .expo uploads android ios dist web-build /XF .env *.log

  Compress-Archive -Path "KL-app-to-send\*" -DestinationPath "KL-app.zip" -Force

The result is about 2 MB. If your zip comes out at hundreds of megabytes then
node_modules got in, and you should do it again.

Delete the KL-app-to-send folder afterwards. It was only scratch.

robocopy prints a number when it finishes. Anything from 0 to 7 means it
worked. Only 8 and above are real failures.

If you have 7-Zip you can do it in one step instead:

  7z a KL-app.zip "KL-electricals-2026-08-26" -xr!node_modules -xr!.expo -xr!uploads -x!backend\.env

WHAT THE OTHER PERSON DOES

Two commands. That is the whole thing.

They need these installed first:

  Node.js, version 18 or newer, from nodejs.org
  MySQL. XAMPP is the easy option. Start MySQL from the XAMPP control panel.

Then:

  1. Unzip the project. Put it somewhere with no spaces or odd characters in
     the folder path.

  2. Open a terminal in that folder and run:

       npm install

     Takes a few minutes and needs an internet connection. This installs both
     halves of the project, the app and the server, in one go.

  3. Then run:

       npm run dev

That is it. The second command works out what still needs doing and does it:

  - writes backend/.env for them, with a randomly generated JWT_SECRET
  - checks MySQL is running, and says exactly what to do if it is not
  - creates the database and all 72 tables
  - creates the 22 staff accounts and an ADMIN001 account
  - loads the 8,885 items and their prices from the two spreadsheets
  - loads the 20 incentive segments
  - starts the server and the app together, in the one terminal

It prints the login password on screen when it finishes.

Every step is skipped if it has already been done, so npm run dev is also the
normal way to start work every day after that. On an already-set-up machine it
just prints a short list of ok lines and starts up.

Output from the two halves is labelled so they can be told apart:

  [api]   the server, and anything to do with the database
  [app]   the bundler that serves the app itself

Press w to open the app in a browser, or scan the QR code with the Expo Go app
on a phone. Ctrl+C stops both halves.

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG, IT SAYS WHAT TO DO

The setup step never stops with a stack trace. It names the problem and the
fix. For example, if MySQL is not running:

  PROBLEM: MySQL is not answering on 127.0.0.1:3306.

  → If you use XAMPP: open the XAMPP Control Panel and press Start next to
    MySQL.
  → Then run this again.

Nothing is left half finished. Running npm run dev again after fixing the
problem picks up where it stopped.

If MySQL has a root password, the message will say so, and they put it into
DB_PASSWORD in backend/.env.

THE OTHER COMMANDS, IF THEY WANT THEM

  npm run setup    do the checking and setting up, without starting anything
  npm run app      start only the app, not the server
  npm run web      start the app and open a browser straight away

And inside the backend folder, the server on its own:

  npm run dev      just the API


They now have a working copy with the real item list and the real staff
structure, but no orders, customers or attendance history.

SENDING YOUR DATA TOO

Only do this if they genuinely need it. It contains real customer names,
balances, and staff records.

On your machine, make a dump of the database. With XAMPP, mysqldump is not on
the PATH, so use the full path to it:

  "C:\xampp\mysql\bin\mysqldump.exe" -u root kl_electricals > kl_data.sql

Send kl_data.sql separately from the zip. On their machine, let npm run dev
finish first so the database exists, then load the dump over it with mysql (not
mysqldump):

  "C:\xampp\mysql\bin\mysql.exe" -u root kl_electricals < kl_data.sql

Two warnings about doing this.

First, the dump carries the staff password hashes with it. Every login on their
copy will work with whatever password it had on yours. Tell them to run
npm run secure-accounts on their copy, which forces a fresh password on every
account.

Second, if you have already run secure-accounts on your own system, then nobody
can sign in to their copy at all until each account picks a new password. Make
them an admin account with create-admin so they have a way in.

QUICK CHECKLIST BEFORE YOU SEND

  [ ] node_modules is not in the zip (the zip should be around 2 MB)
  [ ] backend\.env is not in the zip
  [ ] backend\uploads is not in the zip
  [ ] The two .xlsx spreadsheets ARE in the zip
  [ ] backend\.env.example IS in the zip
  [ ] This guide is in the zip
  [ ] You have decided whether they get your data, and told them either way


===============================================================================
PART 3 - RULES THE SOFTWARE ENFORCES
===============================================================================

These are built in. You will bump into them. They are not bugs.

  - An order must be approved before it can be picked.
  - Goods must be counted by a second person before they can be billed.
  - The invoice bills what was counted, not what was ordered.
  - A delivery needs the receiver's name and a photo.
  - A purchase needs both the billed quantity and the counted quantity.
  - A customer with no type cannot be sold to.
  - An item with no rate cannot be sold.
  - A rate change needs the owner's approval.
  - Only Gaurav can change rates. Sonu cannot even see them.
  - Nobody approves their own order, advance, or leave.
  - The person who sends a stock transfer cannot receive it.
  - The person who declares a cash handover cannot count it.
  - Payments go to the oldest unpaid invoice first, always.
  - A bounced cheque undoes the payment and cancels any discount earned on it.
  - Cancelling an order returns the stock.
  - Deleting is avoided everywhere. Things are marked inactive or reversed, so
    there is always a record of what happened.
  - A salary slip only exists for a finalised month, and does not change after.
  - A password set by somebody else must be changed before the account works.

Some of these will feel like the app being awkward. They are there because
each one is a way money or stock went missing before.

Written and tested against a stand-in, never against real Tally. See 2.8.

The app needs the server. This is deliberate. An earlier version pretended to
work offline and signed people in without checking passwords.
